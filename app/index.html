    Behind the recommendation · how the AI works and its limits

    Three layers. 1) NLP retrieval: TF IDF matches your words to a catalogue product. 2) A transparent chart rule turns the size you own into a starting size. 3) Probabilistic reasoning: a Bayesian belief over sizes gives the confidence, abstains below 50%, and updates when you report real fit. Probabilities are stated modelling assumptions until a pilot measures real outcomes.
